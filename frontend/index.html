AI Apps

Two single-turn apps — one question in, one answer out — and the numbers behind them.

💬 Chat Ask a question, get a short answer. Every exchange is saved. 🌙 Bedtime Story A gentle story built around your child's name and a theme. 📖 Your story stats Every story measured — words, how long it takes to read aloud, reading level, and what they were about.

How it works

  1. Pick an app above.
  2. Sign in with Google when it asks — one tap, no password, nothing to fill in.
  3. Ask for what you want. The first one after a quiet spell can take up to a minute while the server wakes up. After that it is quick.
  4. Every story is measured. Word count, how long it takes to read aloud, how hard it is to read, and what it was about — under each story, and totalled up in Your story stats.

Your stories and questions are saved to your account, and other people using this app cannot see them. The person who runs the app can see who has signed in and what the stories were about. You can sign out from inside either app.
