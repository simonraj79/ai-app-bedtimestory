AI Apps

Two single-turn apps. One question in, one answer out.

💬 Chat Ask a question, get a short answer. Every exchange is saved. 🌙 Bedtime Story A gentle story built around your child's name and a theme.

How it works

  1. Pick an app above.
  2. Sign in with Google when it asks — one tap, no password, nothing to fill in.
  3. Ask for what you want. The first one after a quiet spell can take up to a minute while the server wakes up. After that it is quick.

Everything you make is saved and shown only to you. You can sign out from inside either app.
